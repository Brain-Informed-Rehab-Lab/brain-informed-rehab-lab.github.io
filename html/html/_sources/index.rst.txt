Brain-informed Rehabilitation Lab
=================================

About Us
--------
We are an interdisciplinary research group based in the Department of Neuroscience and Biomedical Engineering at Aalto University. Our mission is to bridge the gap between fundamental systems neuroscience and clinical rehabilitation. We investigate how the human brain controls movement and how it reorganizes (neuroplasticity) after injury, with the ultimate goal of developing scientifically grounded, personalized rehabilitation strategies for individuals with motor impairments.
